How and when we deliverBagaimana dan bila kami menghantar

Last updated: September 2026. We sell digital products and services only — there is no physical shipping. Terakhir dikemas kini: September 2026. Kami hanya menjual produk dan perkhidmatan digital — tiada penghantaran fizikal.

This policy explains how and when Bitara Digital Enterprise ("BDE", "we", "us") delivers what you buy from us. We sell digital products and services only — we do not ship physical goods, and there are no shipping charges.

E-books

E-books are delivered electronically once your payment is confirmed through our payment gateway.

  • How: the e-book and any bonus files are delivered as digital files (PDF) through a download link, shown after payment and/or sent to the email address you entered at checkout.
  • When: normally immediately, and within a few minutes at most after payment is confirmed.
  • Didn't receive it? Check your spam/junk folder first, and make sure the email address you entered was correct. If it still hasn't arrived within 24 hours, contact us with your name, email, and order details and we will send it to you within 1 business day.

Training

  • Where: on-site at your premises anywhere in Malaysia, or live online.
  • When: the date and time are agreed with you in writing after we confirm your enquiry. Travel and venue arrangements for on-site sessions follow your quotation.
  • Materials: training materials are shared digitally before or after the session, as agreed.

Web & system development

  • How: delivered digitally — deployed to your hosting and domain, with a handover of access and documentation.
  • When: timelines and milestones are set out in your quotation. Delivery is complete on handover.

Delays

If something outside our control delays a delivery (for example a payment or email provider outage, illness, or other unforeseen events), we will let you know as soon as we can and agree the next steps with you.

Something not delivered? Reach out — or WhatsApp .

Related policies

If you need a refund or replacement, please see our Refund Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products and services change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

Dasar ini menerangkan bagaimana dan bila Bitara Digital Enterprise ("BDE", "kami") menghantar apa yang anda beli daripada kami. Kami hanya menjual produk dan perkhidmatan digital — kami tidak menghantar barangan fizikal, dan tiada caj penghantaran.

E-book

E-book dihantar secara elektronik selepas pembayaran anda disahkan melalui gerbang pembayaran kami.

  • Bagaimana: e-book dan sebarang fail bonus dihantar sebagai fail digital (PDF) melalui pautan muat turun, dipaparkan selepas pembayaran dan/atau dihantar ke alamat e-mel yang anda masukkan semasa pembayaran.
  • Bila: biasanya serta-merta, dan paling lewat dalam beberapa minit selepas pembayaran disahkan.
  • Tak terima? Semak folder spam/junk anda dahulu, dan pastikan alamat e-mel yang dimasukkan adalah betul. Jika masih belum tiba dalam tempoh 24 jam, hubungi kami dengan nama, e-mel, dan butiran pesanan anda dan kami akan menghantarnya dalam tempoh 1 hari bekerja.

Latihan

  • Di mana: secara bersemuka di premis anda di mana-mana sahaja di Malaysia, atau secara langsung dalam talian.
  • Bila: tarikh dan masa dipersetujui bersama anda secara bertulis selepas kami mengesahkan pertanyaan anda. Pengaturan perjalanan dan tempat bagi sesi bersemuka mengikut sebut harga anda.
  • Bahan: bahan latihan dikongsi secara digital sebelum atau selepas sesi, seperti dipersetujui.

Pembangunan web & sistem

  • Bagaimana: dihantar secara digital — dipasang pada hosting dan domain anda, bersama serahan akses dan dokumentasi.
  • Bila: garis masa dan pencapaian ditetapkan dalam sebut harga anda. Penghantaran lengkap apabila serahan dibuat.

Kelewatan

Jika sesuatu di luar kawalan kami melewatkan penghantaran (contohnya gangguan penyedia pembayaran atau e-mel, sakit, atau kejadian tidak dijangka lain), kami akan memaklumkan anda secepat mungkin dan bersetuju dengan langkah seterusnya bersama anda.

Ada yang belum sampai? Hubungi kami — atau WhatsApp .

Dasar berkaitan

Jika anda memerlukan bayaran balik atau penggantian, sila lihat Dasar Bayaran Balik kami.

Perubahan kepada dasar ini

Kami mungkin mengemas kini dasar ini apabila produk dan perkhidmatan kami berubah. Tarikh "terakhir dikemas kini" di atas mencerminkan semakan terkini.
