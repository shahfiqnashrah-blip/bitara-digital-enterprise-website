FounderPengasas

Ts. Shahfiq Nashrah

Founder — Bitara Digital EnterprisePengasas — Bitara Digital Enterprise

With almost a decade of experience as an IT professional at multinational companies — including Quantum (US-based), PETRONAS, and Tribal Group (UK-based) — Shahfiq founded Bitara Digital Enterprise to bring that same rigor to Malaysian businesses, both in the systems he builds and the training he delivers. Dengan hampir sedekad pengalaman sebagai profesional IT di syarikat multinasional — termasuk Quantum (berpangkalan di AS), PETRONAS, dan Tribal Group (berpangkalan di UK) — Shahfiq mengasaskan Bitara Digital Enterprise untuk membawa ketelitian yang sama kepada perniagaan di Malaysia, dalam sistem yang dibina mahupun latihan yang disampaikan.

His startup ventures have been selected for several national innovation accelerator programs — SME Bank's BID-it Accelerator (2017), UTP's UToPIA Innovation Accelerator (2024), and MRANTI's GAP (2025) — a track record of building and validating real products under real constraints, not just presenting slides. Syarikat permulaannya turut dipilih untuk menyertai beberapa program accelerator inovasi peringkat kebangsaan — BID-it Accelerator SME Bank (2017), UToPIA Innovation Accelerator UTP (2024), dan GAP MRANTI (2025) — rekod trek membina dan mengesahkan produk sebenar di bawah kekangan sebenar, bukan sekadar mempersembahkan slaid.

Today he works as a freelance consultant and trainer in web/system development and applied AI, and has served as an External Examiner for Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP)'s Technopreneurship Team Project (TTP) since 2025. Kini beliau berkhidmat sebagai perunding dan jurulatih bebas dalam bidang pembangunan web/sistem dan AI gunaan, serta telah berkhidmat sebagai Pemeriksa Luar bagi Technopreneurship Team Project (TTP), Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP) sejak 2025.
